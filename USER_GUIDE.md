# PHANTOM S23 — Operator Guide

## One-time setup
1. Open the `mobile` folder in PowerShell.
2. Type `npm install`, press Enter. Then `npm run setup`.

## Every session
1. Type `npm run dev`. The browser opens the app.
2. Click **Enable camera** and allow the camera.
3. Wait for "Hand tracking ready".
4. Stand 1–1.5 m from the laptop, light on your face and hands, phone in one hand.
5. **Show your open palm** to the camera and hold still → the hologram appears.

## Hand controls
- **Point** with the index finger to move the glowing ring.
- **Tap**: touch thumb and index finger together quickly, then open.
- **Scroll**: pinch, move your hand up or down, open.
- **Close an app**: pinch and hold still until the yellow ring is full.
- **Back**: swipe your open hand quickly left or right.
- **Hide the hologram**: make a fist and hold 1 second.
- **Type**: point at a key and pinch. The send button adds the message (simulated — nothing is really sent).

## If gestures feel wrong
Press **C** and follow the 3 steps (open hand → pinch → apply). Press **D** to see what the camera detects.

## Recording
1. Press **S**, set *Output resolution* to 1920x1080, position the hologram. Press **S** again.
2. Press **P** (clean presentation view).
3. Press **R**. After the countdown it records. Press **R** again to stop.
4. Check the preview, click **Save** — the .webm file goes to Downloads.
5. Press **P** to leave presentation mode.

## Problems
- No picture: close Zoom/Teams/Camera app, click Retry.
- Hand not seen: more light on the hand, palm toward the camera.
- Slow: Settings → Effects → Visual quality preset → *low*.
